Hint Health founders, Zak Holdsworth and Graham Melcher, sought solutions to the US healthcare system that would uniformly benefit clinicians and patients. In their research, Zak and Graham saw the need to restore the integrity of primary care, eliminating the insurance fee-for-service infrastructure from routine care delivery and payment, and discovered direct primary care as the best solution. They started their journey by building the operating system for direct primary care (DPC).
