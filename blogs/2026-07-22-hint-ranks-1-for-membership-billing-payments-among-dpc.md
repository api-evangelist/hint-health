For more than a decade, Hint has helped Direct Primary Care practices grow with software purpose-built for memberships, billing, and payments. That long-standing focus was recently acknowledged in My DPC Story’s 2026 Battle of the Support Stack , where Hint earned 1st place in the Membership Billing & Payments category, receiving the most votes from practicing DPC physicians. Hint was also recognized across several additional categories, reflecting Hint’s strength across its EMR-related capabilities.
